# Acceptable Use Policy

**Argus** is a security reconnaissance framework. It is designed, built, and
released for the sole purpose of **authorized security testing** — including
but not limited to bug bounty programs, penetration tests, red team
engagements, CTFs, security research on infrastructure you own, and education.

## Authorized Use

You may use Argus **only** against systems for which you have explicit,
documented, and current authorization to test. This includes:

- Bug bounty programs where the target is demonstrably in scope
- Signed penetration testing engagements
- CTF competitions and lab environments
- Infrastructure and applications you personally own or operate
- Academic research conducted under an institution's IRB or ethics review

## Prohibited Use

You may **not** use Argus to:

- Scan, probe, or test any system without prior written authorization
- Target government, military, critical infrastructure, healthcare, or
  financial systems without an explicit legal engagement
- Conduct denial-of-service, resource-exhaustion, or mass-scanning activity
- Violate any local, state, national, or international law
- Violate the terms of service of any bug bounty platform or program
- Facilitate harm against any individual or organization
